MOTION SYSTEM

Motion should feel **designed**, not randomly animated.

Use GSAP extensively where appropriate.

Use:

* GSAP timelines
* ScrollTrigger
* smooth scrolling with Lenis
* carefully choreographed entrance sequences
* velocity-based interactions
* parallax
* pinned sections
* shared transitions
* magnetic interactions
* hover states
* cursor interactions
* SVG animation
* text reveals
* clip-path transitions
* camera movement
* shader effects where appropriate

Motion should establish hierarchy.

Do not animate everything.

The visitor should always know what deserves attention.
