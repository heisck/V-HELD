# Typography & Font Direction

## Direction & Rules

Do not automatically default to **Inter**, **Space Grotesk**, **Instrument Serif**, or other trendy AI-generated font combinations.

Choose typography intentionally based on V-HELD's actual brand identity and design system (a credible, warm, African community development and volunteer organisation).

### Mandatory Criteria:
* **Clear hierarchy**: Explicit scale between display titles, section headings, subheadings, body text, and captions.
* **Strong readability**: High legibility across body paragraphs, forms, and informational sections on all display types.
* **Consistent weights**: Defined set of font weights used systematically across UI states and hierarchy tiers.
* **Consistent line heights**: Proportional line heights that prevent text collisions and maintain vertical rhythm.
* **Appropriate letter spacing**: Calibrated tracking (slightly tighter on large headings, neutral/open on body and captions).
* **Proper responsive behavior**: Fluid or breakpoint-calibrated type scaling that avoids awkward wrapping or clipping on mobile.

Never use trendy font combinations simply because they are popular in AI-generated interfaces. Every typeface selection must support the organisation's credibility and warmth.
