# Signature Interactions ("The Wow Moment")

## Concept & Purpose

The V-HELD web experience must feature at least one genuinely memorable signature interaction—an interactive element that elevates the site beyond a conventional NGO page into an engaging, memorable digital experience.

---

## Guidelines for Signature Interactions

* **Must Serve the Mission**: The signature interaction must communicate V-HELD's actual mission (e.g., an interactive Ghana community impact map, a dynamic volunteer journey visualizer, or an interactive cultural orientation guide).
* **No Gimmicks**: Do not include gratuitous 3D models or floating effects that have no conceptual connection to Ghanaian community development.
* **Tactile & Responsive**: The interaction must respond naturally to user gestures (drag, hover, click, scrub) with instant feedback and 60 FPS performance.
* **Graceful Fallback**: If hardware acceleration is low or screen size is constrained, the interaction must gracefully simplify into an accessible, informative view without breaking layout or usability.
